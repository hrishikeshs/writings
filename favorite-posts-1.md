# Some of my favorite posts

**published on : Wednesday, March 19, 2025**

From time to time, I come across terrific blogs written by other
people. In the old days, I used to maintain an org-mode text file
with the blog post titles and relied mainly on google search
if I wanted to look up the contents.

Now that google search has gone to shit and there is like 1000% AI slop
on the web, I have become a link-hoader: I bookmark links and also
submit them to archive.org for posterity.

From time to time, I will write posts here such as this one where I link
to some of the stuff I'm reading/appreciating.

Just thought I'd share this with the readers of my blog. Enjoy!
