# What is a Transformer?

Transformer is an architecture which is very efficient to compute the function (AI model), that can compute arbitrary curves in the higher dimensional spaces.

**The _Transformer_ is the architecture for building our AI model**

- Just like polynomials are one kind of a function family, and Fourier series are another,
- Transformers are a specific **recipe** for building extremely expressive functions

The _transformer_ defines:
- How inputs are represented (embeddings)
- How information flows(layers of attention + feed-forward networks)
- How outputs are generated(probability distributions over tokens)

The transformer doesn't compute anything. It's not the AI model. It defines the _form of the function (the curve family)_, and gives us an efficient way to train and evaluate it.
